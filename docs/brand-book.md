# EventRecruit Brand Book

## Mission
EventRecruit is an India-wide people operations platform for events, exhibitions, retail stores, launches, roadshows, pop-ups, and brand activations. The product helps businesses find and manage verified people wherever work is happening.

## Positioning
- **Core promise:** the right people for events and retail activations across India.
- **Primary audiences:** brands, exhibitors, agencies, retail/store teams, and event talent.
- **Primary focus:** people, trust, availability, location coverage, and clean operational records.
- **Product tone:** direct, calm, useful, and people-first. Avoid inflated marketing claims and vague career language.

## Visual System
- **Palette:** use `--background`, `--panel`, `--surface`, `--ink`, `--foreground`, `--muted`, `--line`, `--accent`, and `--accent-2` from `src/app/globals.css`.
- **Primary color role:** `--accent` is for main actions, verification, coverage, and active states.
- **Secondary color role:** `--accent-2` is for map pins, activity highlights, commission, ratings, or urgent demand signals.
- **Shape:** cards, buttons, inputs, maps, and panels use 7-8px radii. Avoid oversized rounded containers.
- **Imagery:** use realistic Indian event and retail operations imagery: booths, badges, promoters, counters, stores, malls, launches, tablets, check-in desks, and on-ground teams.

## Typography And Layout
- Use the existing system sans-serif stack.
- Lead public pages with a direct promise: people for events and retail activations across India.
- Keep headings short, operational, and specific.
- Use map, city, and staffing-need signals to make national coverage feel concrete.
- Dashboards and forms should stay dense, scannable, and task-focused.

## Components
- **Buttons:** primary buttons start a hiring or sign-up action; secondary buttons guide alternate paths such as joining as talent.
- **Badges:** badges mark verification, coverage, workflow state, or small contextual labels.
- **Panels:** use panels for individual cards, forms, maps, and bounded tools. Avoid nesting cards inside cards.
- **Maps:** coverage maps are visual proof of reach, not decoration. Use recognizable geographic outlines from local, license-safe assets, never abstract placeholder polygons, and pair city pins with counts or operational context. Record source context when a map asset is added.
- **Forms:** labels should be concrete and short. Error or status messages must be visible near the form header.

## Voice Examples
- Use: "Find people", "Join as talent", "Events and retail activations across India", "Tell us where", "Match verified people", "Track placements".
- Avoid: "Unlock your potential", "Revolutionize events", "Seamless ecosystem", "World-class staffing solution".
